# OniGame v0.40.71 - Terra Gem Attunement

- Terra gems no longer grant spirit power instantly.
- Interacting with a gem starts a personal attunement channel.
- Base completion is about 15 seconds, with repeated SHIFT skill checks.
- Normal success adds progress; GREAT adds a larger bonus and can reduce completion into the low teens.
- Failure removes progress and produces a loud explosion cue / particle burst.
- Moving more than 5 blocks away, becoming downed/dead, escaping, or entering revival parkour interrupts attunement.
- Interrupted attunement retains 75% of the current progress.
- Progress and completion are per-player and per-gem; multiple players may work the same gem independently.
- Completed gems still award +1 spirit power and the existing SP reward.
